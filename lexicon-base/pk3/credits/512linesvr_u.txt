Title                   : 512 Linedefs of /vr/
Filename                : 512linesvr.wad
Release date            : 07/03/2022
Author                  : Various
Email Address           : [redacted]
Other Files By Author   : 400 Minutes of /vr/, HFFM, Ad Mortem
Misc. Author Info       : /vr/ - Retro Games, Doom General

Description             : 32 maps constructed using no more than 512 linedefs
                          in each map. All maps are designed in Boom format
                          (complevel 9) and use ultratex.wad for textures,
                          also utilizing UMAPINFO to supply special actions.
                          Also contains a separate WAD for ports that do not
                          support widescreen HUDs. 

Additional Credits to   : Level Design: Redead-ITA, EnragedEggplant,

                          NepNep, Jarmo, Yuji Sakai

                          anons

* Construction *

Base                    : New from scratch
Build Time              : 5 weeks
Editor(s) used          : Ultimate Doombuilder, Slade
Known Bugs              : MAP07 will not function properly outside of
                          complevel 9 or a ZDoom port, MAP22's MIDI may have
                          issues looping, may still contain texture
                          misalignments or other small errors
May Not Run With        : Any non-UMAPINFO compatible port
Tested With             : DSDA, Woof!, GZDoom
