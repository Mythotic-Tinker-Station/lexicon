* Description: *
===========================================================================

A long time ago in the Doom community, there was an interesting project called Community Chest. Some people in the Doom community felt that this project needed some sort of spiritual successor. And thus, Community Trunk was born. Community Trunk is a vanilla limit-removing megawad that contains 32 maps.
