[2]	How to play SCIENTIST2
******************************
If you're using a source port you can start playing sci2 without reading this chapter.
If you're using ZDoom for example you can simply drag and drop SCI2.wad on the ZDoom.exe and start playing.
Bear in mind that SCI2.wad contains an internal dehacked file.

As mentioned before it is possible to play the wad without a source port under MSDOS. Here's how:

-unzip sci2.zip in your doom2 directory
-copy the files from the scivanil folder to your main doom directory (where doom2.wad and doom2.exe) are.
-run scisetup.bat
-type "sci2 -file sci2.wad"

Note that this will not harm your original files.

[3]	Story
*************
I changed the story a bit. Here it goes:

When early humans ate from the forbidden tree of knowledge of good and evil it brought pain and misery in the world, cursed
the ground and man became evil. By gaining this knowledge god decided man should no longer be allowed to be able 
to live forever. Thus turning the tree of life (immorality) into a forbidden tree.

As science continues to improve it's understanding on life, origine, death and immortality it seems as if mankind
again is at the point eating from a forbidden fruit.

In our story a scientist is near the discovery of human immortality. He believes we don't need god to achieve immortality
but can gain it through science.

god is not pleased with this disobedience and as before he decides to turn mankind's home into a less comfortable place.
As the scientist continues his work earth is hit by natural disasters and strange monsters are taking over.
But the man of science is not impressed by all this and ignores all of god's warnings.

It seems there is only one way to prevent the earth from becoming an even greater hell for humans;
to kill the scientist.

but be warned: he appears to be carring a portable teleporter enabling him to escape you.


...


Okay, It's a lame story. Please don't take it to seriously; I have no intention to offend anyone with this wad.

[9]	Notes
*************
NOTE 1:	SCI.wad SCIsetup.bat (or any of the other unzipped files) will not alter you original 
	doom2.wad or doom2.exe; using scientist2 is perfectly safe.

Note 2: As mentioned earlier The first two episodes (map 1-11) of sci2.wad have been released 
	earlier (October 2002) in a wad called sci.wad. These episodes have remained virtually unaltered 
	in sci2 making sci.wad a bit obsolete.
	Demos and savegames made for sci.wad should work fine with sci2.wad (perhaps not for map07).

Note 3: If you have some reviews or demos please let me know they're very much appreciated!

Note 4: For some reason some people seem to think the wad has a anti-Soviet plot. No such plot was intended.

Note 5: I tested the deathmatch maps with ZDaemon and I found a bug; The crossbow has some trouble firing.
	Now, I have no doubt this has to do with the alterations I made with DeHackEd but the crossbow works fine with
	ZDoom and vanilla Doom2 so I blame the problem on ZDaemon! I got it working right in ZDaemon at one point but then
	when I tried it in ZDoom and vanilla Doom it didn't work right so I decided to go with the version you have now. 
	If you want to have to crossbow working fine in ZDaemon you should run sci2.wad with SCI2_zdaemon_crossbow_fix.wad.	

NOTE 6: As said Sci2.wad is made to be able to run with the vanilla doom2.exe. This means I designed the maps with the idea
	that the player can't jump. So don't jump.

NOTE 7: Please do not take the story to seriously; 
	There is no quarrel between science and god 
	(...since one of them does not exist).

I Hope I've informed you enough and you'll enjoy playing the wad,
